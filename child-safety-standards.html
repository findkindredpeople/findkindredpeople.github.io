Kindred Child Safety Standards

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Zero tolerance: Kindred prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards apply to all users, content, and interactions on Kindred.

1. Prohibited conduct and content

Kindred strictly prohibits content or behavior that sexually exploits, abuses, endangers, grooms, coerces, traffics, or otherwise harms a child or minor. This includes attempts to solicit sexual content from minors, sexual extortion, grooming, facilitation of child trafficking, and the creation, possession, sharing, promotion, or distribution of CSAM.

2. Reporting concerns

Users should report suspected child-safety violations through the reporting features available in Kindred. Reports are reviewed and appropriate action may be taken against content or accounts that violate these standards.

3. Enforcement

When Kindred identifies content or conduct that violates these standards, we may remove the content, restrict or terminate accounts, preserve relevant information where legally required, and take other appropriate safety measures.

4. CSAM response

Kindred does not permit CSAM. When we become aware of suspected CSAM, we take action consistent with applicable law and our safety procedures, including removing prohibited material and making reports to appropriate authorities where legally required.

5. Compliance with child-safety laws

Kindred is committed to complying with applicable child-safety laws and regulations and to cooperating with valid legal requests from competent authorities.

6. Safety contact

Questions from Google Play or competent authorities concerning Kindred's child-safety practices should be directed to the child-safety contact designated for Kindred in Google Play Console.

7. Scope

These standards form part of Kindred's safety rules. Violations may result in immediate action to protect users and the community.
