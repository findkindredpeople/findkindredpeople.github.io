What to check before choosing

A free event can still cost a bus fare. A one-hour group can take two hours of your evening. The calculation adds session time to the complete return journey, and visit fees to transport costs. It compares these totals with the limits you entered.

What the three results mean

  • Fits your entered limits: the totals are within your limits and all three joining checks are confirmed. Ask about booking and the next date before going.
  • More to check: at least one number or joining detail is missing. The tool never treats an unknown cost as free.
  • Does not fit yet: a total exceeds your limit or you marked a joining requirement “No”. Look at the stated reason; a different session or route might change it.

This comparison cannot verify an organiser, availability, accessibility, safety or whether you will enjoy a group. There is no hidden popularity or compatibility score.

A question to send the organiser

Hi, I would like to join [activity] on [date]. Is it open to newcomers? Could you confirm the total first-visit cost, the finish time and whether I need to book? I also wanted to check [language or access question].

Keep a copy before leaving

Results and notes disappear when you reload or close the page. Copy, download or print the current comparison if you want to keep it. Do not put home addresses, private contact details or information about other people in your notes.
