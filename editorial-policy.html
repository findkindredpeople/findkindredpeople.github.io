EDITORIAL POLICY

Useful guidance, written with care.

Kindred publishes original, practical information for people building social lives in new places.

Updated: 31 October 2026

Author and responsibility

Kindred is published by Nadiia Honda, the project creator. Guides and tools may use AI-assisted drafting. The material offers practical suggestions; it does not replace legal, medical, psychological or emergency advice.

How topics are selected

Topics begin with a concrete reader problem: starting a conversation, finding recurring groups, arranging a safer meeting or maintaining a new friendship. Each article should offer actions a reader can realistically try, explain trade-offs and avoid guarantees about personal outcomes.

Originality and review

Articles are written specifically for Kindred. Before publication, they are checked for clarity, internal consistency, respectful language, privacy risks, misleading promises and overlap with existing guides. Demonstration stories and interface examples are labeled as examples.

AI assistance, examples and sources

AI is used to help draft, organise and revise some guides, message templates and planning tools. This does not mean that an expert reviewed every article or that the publisher personally attended each activity mentioned. Illustrative situations are examples, not testimonials or reported interviews. Local resource guides link to primary sources and give a check date; readers should confirm current schedules, fees and availability with the organiser. The planner and social-step tools use fixed suggestions, not live event data or predictions of social outcomes. Social Dice filters steps by the selected time and energy; time estimates exclude travel and booking. Daily records user-marked completions in the current browser. The preferences tool uses the choices entered on the page to suggest questions and an invitation; it does not calculate a matching score. Illustrative schedules are examples, not a promise that a group, venue or person is available.

Safety and sensitive topics

Safety guidance favors public first meetings, control of personal information, independent transport and respect for consent. General wellbeing articles include a clear boundary when professional or emergency support may be appropriate.

Corrections and updates

Publication and update dates appear on guides. Material is reviewed when site features change, when guidance becomes outdated or when a factual or safety concern is identified. Readers can send corrections privately through the Contact page. Reproducible technical issues that contain no personal information may also be reported through the public project issue tracker.

Advertising independence

Advertising does not determine article topics, conclusions or the order in which guides are recommended. Sponsored material, if ever published, will be clearly labeled. Ads are not intentionally placed so that they resemble navigation, editorial recommendations, download buttons or community actions.

Contact

For editorial corrections, accessibility concerns or private feedback, use the Contact page. The public Kindred issue tracker is reserved for technical problems that can be described without personal information. Never publish account data, private correspondence, identity documents, exact addresses or other sensitive information in a public issue.
