OUR MISSION

Belonging should not depend on luck.

Kindred is a community project for people who have moved to a new city and want to meet others through shared interests, small groups and low-pressure plans.

Why Kindred exists

Moving can be exciting and lonely at the same time. A new place may offer work, study or adventure, while everyday belonging still takes time. Kindred is designed to shorten the distance between “I am new here” and “I know people here.”

The project favors manageable social steps, clear activities and public meeting places. Kindred is not a dating service. Its purpose is adult friendship, community discovery and practical support for people building a social life in a new place.

What Kindred offers

  • Practical guides: step-by-step articles about meeting people, following up, finding recurring groups, handling loneliness after a move and building sustainable friendships.
  • Adult community tools: profiles based on city, interests, languages, availability and communication rhythm, with private messaging only after mutual connection.
  • A community forum: a developing space for local questions, activities, language exchange and friendship topics.
  • Safety guidance: clear advice about privacy, boundaries, public first meetings, reporting and blocking.

Who Kindred is for

Kindred is written primarily for adults who are new to a city or country, rebuilding a social life after change, working remotely, learning a language or simply looking for more compatible friendships. The guides are designed to be useful even if a reader never creates a community account.

Creator and editorial responsibility

Nadiia Honda is the creator and editor of Kindred, an independent project dedicated to helping people build meaningful friendships and feel more at home in a new city.

She created Kindred to offer practical, thoughtful guidance for the moments that often feel the most difficult: meeting people after moving, starting natural conversations, finding welcoming groups and turning new acquaintances into lasting friendships.

Nadiia develops Kindred with an emphasis on realistic actions, personal boundaries, privacy and safer public meetings. Some guides and tools are drafted and revised with AI assistance, as described in the editorial policy. The project avoids promises of instant connection and instead focuses on the small, repeatable steps that help trust and belonging grow over time.

Kindred is an independent project. Nadiia continues to develop its guides and adult community tools in response to reader and member feedback. Editorial content is kept separate from advertising decisions, and articles are updated when guidance, site features or safety information materially changes.

Read the editorial policy for information about originality, corrections, safety review and advertising independence.

Kindred Community helps adults discover profiles, exchange connection requests and message after mutual acceptance. It is not a dating or emergency service.
