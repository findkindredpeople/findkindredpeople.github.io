OUR MISSION

Belonging should not depend on luck.

Kindred is a community project for people who have moved to a new city and want to meet others through shared interests, small groups and low-pressure plans.

Why Kindred exists

Moving can be exciting and lonely at the same time. A new place may offer work, study or adventure, while everyday belonging still takes time. Kindred is designed to shorten the distance between “I am new here” and “I know people here.”

The project favors groups of four to six people, clear activities and public meeting places. It is not a dating service and Social Dice is not gambling. It is a playful way to discover possible social connections without money, prizes or wagering.

What we are building

  • Interest-based discovery for newcomers.
  • Life stories that help people feel less alone.
  • A practical forum for local questions and plans.
  • Guides grounded in realistic, respectful social habits.

Creator and editorial responsibility

Kindred was created by Nadiia Honda, who writes and reviews the project’s practical guides. The goal is to turn broad advice about belonging into specific, respectful actions that newcomers can test in real life.

Read the editorial policy for information about originality, corrections, safety review and advertising independence.

Kindred is a public preview. Matching, profiles, group requests, stories and forum publishing are demonstrations; they do not currently connect visitors or publish submissions.
