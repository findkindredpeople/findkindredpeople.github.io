    Your last seven days on this device

      Use the record as a reminder

      Mark a step only after you have done it. One completion is counted per local calendar day; choosing another suggestion does not mark that new suggestion as completed. You can undo a mistaken mark. An unmarked day only means there is no record, not that you failed to make progress.

      Your goal and time shape the suggestion. The checklist and wording are options to adapt. Availability, travel and another person’s willingness still need checking.
