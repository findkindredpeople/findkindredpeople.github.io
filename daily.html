How to use Kindred Daily

Kindred Daily is designed for small, repeatable social actions rather than big goals. Choose one suggestion that feels realistic, adapt it to your situation and stop if it does not feel safe or appropriate. Repeating a manageable action—such as returning to the same public place, sending a follow-up message or making one simple invitation—can make social contact easier to maintain over time.

The completion counter is stored only on this device in your browser. Kindred Daily does not diagnose loneliness, social anxiety or any health condition, and it is not a substitute for professional support. If a challenge involves meeting someone new, prefer a public place, keep control of your own transport and avoid sharing sensitive personal information too early.
