What Social Dice is for

Social Dice is a lightweight idea generator for days when you want to do something social but do not want to plan from scratch. Your selected time and social energy help narrow the suggestion, but the result is not a prediction, personality assessment or professional recommendation. Treat it as a prompt you can change, skip or repeat.

Choose the lowest-pressure version that still feels useful. A ten-minute message, a short visit to a recurring public activity or a simple invitation can be more sustainable than forcing a large social event. For first meetings, use public places and keep personal details private until trust develops.
