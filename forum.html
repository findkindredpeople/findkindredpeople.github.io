How to use the Kindred forum

The forum is designed for useful, low-pressure conversations between adults who are building a social life in a new city or country. You can ask about recurring activities, language exchange, first-meeting ideas, friendship after moving or practical ways to feel less isolated.

A helpful post gives enough context to answer without exposing private information. City-level location, preferred activity and general availability are usually enough. Avoid phone numbers, exact home addresses, workplace schedules, identity documents and private information about children or other people.

If you want to turn a discussion into an in-person plan, use Kindred Meetups and follow the Safety and Community Guidelines. Community posts are user-generated and are not endorsements or professional advice.

Forum FAQ

What makes a good forum question?

A useful question gives city-level context, the kind of activity or friendship you want, your general availability and one clear thing you need help with.

Can I move from a forum topic to a meetup?

Yes. When a discussion becomes a real plan, create a Kindred Meetup instead of posting exact private details in the forum.

What should stay private?

Phone numbers, exact addresses, work schedules, identity documents, private information about children and sensitive personal history should not be posted publicly.
