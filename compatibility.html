How to use this tool

Friendship Compatibility is a conversation starter, not a scientific test and not a score of whether two people should be friends. It helps you notice practical preferences such as how often you like to make plans, whether you prefer one-to-one or group settings, and whether conversation or shared activities feel easier.

Different answers are not automatically a problem. They can simply show where a little planning helps—for example, agreeing on how often to meet or choosing an activity that suits both people. Do not use the result to label another person or make assumptions about their personality, health or intentions.
