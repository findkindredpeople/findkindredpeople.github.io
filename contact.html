CONTACT

Talk to the people behind Kindred.

Questions, feedback and safety reports help us build a more useful community.

General feedback

Contact the project owner through the public GitHub profile or open a Kindred project issue for general feedback and reproducible technical problems. Never publish private addresses, identity documents, authentication details or other sensitive information in a public issue.

Safety and content reports

If public project content appears threatening, hateful, sexually explicit, fraudulent or illegal, report it through the project issue tracker without repeating sensitive material. For urgent danger, contact local emergency services rather than waiting for an online response.

Account and privacy requests

GitHub Issues are public and are not suitable for account-data requests. Do not publish names, email addresses, private correspondence, passwords, authentication codes or identity documents there. Until a private support channel is introduced, avoid submitting personal data through the issue tracker; the site will publish a dedicated private contact before accepting sensitive requests.
