CONTACT

Talk to the people behind Kindred.

Questions, feedback and safety reports help us build a more useful community.

Kindred is created and edited by Nadiia Honda. The email below is the primary private contact for the project.

General feedback

Email nadiiahonda34@gmail.com for private feedback, privacy requests and accessibility problems. For reproducible technical issues that contain no personal information, use the public project issue tracker.

Safety and content reports

Signed-in members can use the private report and block controls inside Kindred Community. You may also email nadiiahonda34@gmail.com for a real content or safety concern. Do not include passwords, identity documents, exact addresses or unnecessary sensitive information. For urgent danger, contact local emergency services.

Account and privacy requests

For access, correction or deletion questions, email the private contact above from the address associated with your account. Include “Privacy Request” in the subject line.

What to include

For general feedback, describe the page or feature involved and what you expected to happen. For privacy or account requests, write from the email associated with your account when possible. For safety concerns, include only the information needed to understand the issue and avoid sending passwords, identity documents or unnecessary sensitive details.

Response and scope

Kindred is an independent project. Messages are reviewed as capacity allows, with privacy and safety matters prioritized. The contact email is not an emergency service and cannot provide urgent medical, legal or law-enforcement assistance.
