    Before your first visit

    1. Check the date, registration and available places on the organiser’s page.
    2. Allow for the whole session and your return journey. Ask beforehand if you want a shorter visit.
    3. Prepare one simple question: “Can I join on my own for the first time?”

    One useful first-visit question

    “Hello, I would like to take part for the first time. Is there still a place on the published date? Do I need to register or bring anything?”

    A recurring activity can give you a chance to meet the same people again. One manageable first visit is enough to begin.
