SAFETY

Kind connections need clear boundaries.

These guidelines explain safer conduct for the current public preview and for any community features that may be enabled in the future.

Meet safely

  • Choose a busy public place for a first meeting.
  • Arrange your own transport and tell someone where you are going.
  • Do not share your home address, financial details or identity documents.
  • Leave whenever you feel uncomfortable. You do not owe anyone an explanation.

Community standards

Do not post harassment, threats, hate speech, sexual exploitation, scams, impersonation, instructions for wrongdoing or content that violates another person's privacy. Do not pressure people for romantic, sexual, financial or political engagement.

Stories and photographs

Share only material you have the right to publish. Ask permission before posting identifiable photographs or private stories about another person. Avoid exact live locations and details that could expose a vulnerable person.

Moderation

The current preview does not publish visitor submissions or operate a staffed in-product moderation queue. Preview reports remain in the visitor’s browser. Real content or safety concerns should be emailed privately to nadiiahonda34@gmail.com.

Emergency: Kindred is not an emergency service. Contact local emergency services if someone may be in immediate danger.
