    One useful visit starts with a few checks

    Open the organiser’s page before travelling. Confirm the date, available places, full cost, language and access you need. A listed session window is not a promise that you can arrive late or leave early; ask if you need a shorter visit. The comparison tool adds your return journey and other costs.

    What “source checked” means

    We read the linked official pages on 20 September 2026. We have not attended or independently inspected these sessions. Organisers may change or cancel them, and a place is not reserved by making a Kindred plan. For recurring formats without a future published date, we show the schedule and ask you to confirm the next session. Once a one-off date is past, the interactive list hides it.

    We label missing prices and languages as unknown. English wording on Kindred does not mean an activity is in English. The first-visit ideas are Kindred suggestions, separate from the organiser’s information. Content preparation used AI assistance; see our editorial policy.

    This collection is a starting point, not a complete Berlin directory. You can also search the official city resources in our Berlin guide. Tell us about an incorrect date or a useful local programme.
