PRIVACY

Your story belongs to you.

This policy describes how the Kindred editorial site and adult community application process information.

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Accounts and private account data

The community application uses Google Sign-In and Firebase. When you sign in, Kindred receives your Google account identifier, display name, email address and profile image if available. Your email address is stored in a private account record and is not displayed to other members.

Community profiles

You may create an adult community profile containing a display name, optional profile photo, city, country, introduction, interests, languages, availability and visibility choice. Profile photos are resized in your browser before they are stored with the profile. Signed-in members can view active profiles, including their photos. Do not include an exact address, phone number, workplace, identity documents, medical information or information about children.

Connections, messages, voice messages, calls, blocks and reports

Kindred stores connection requests and their status. Private messaging is available only after the recipient accepts a request. Text and voice messages are stored with the private conversation and are intended to be visible only to the connected members who can access that conversation. Voice recording requires browser permission to use the microphone. Blocks are used to hide profiles and prevent new messages. Reports include the selected reason, details, the reporting and reported account identifiers, status and submission time; reports are visible to the reporter and authorized moderators.

Video-call invitations create a private room through the third-party Jitsi Meet service. Kindred stores the room link and basic call status in the conversation so the participants can join or respond. The actual video and audio call is handled by Jitsi Meet under its own privacy terms; Kindred does not store the call video stream in Firestore.

Favorites, matching preferences and friendship progress

You may save favorites, choose friendship and communication preferences, propose public meeting plans, and record whether you met or called again, remain in contact, or found a match useful. These settings and check-ins may be stored with your account or connection record and used to organize the Community experience and improve your future Kindred recommendations. Private check-in answers are not intended to be shown to the other member unless the feature explicitly describes a shared result. You can change eligible profile and preference information in the Community interface.

Public planner and guide search

The friendship planner and guide search work in your browser without a Kindred account. Planner selections, completion ticks and guide-search terms are not sent to the Kindred community database or saved by these tools after the page closes. You can deliberately copy, print or download the current plan; those copies are under your control.

Browser storage

Kindred may use localStorage to remember interface and privacy choices. This information remains on your device until you clear the site data in your browser.

Hosting and technical logs

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, and application data is processed by Google Firebase. These providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, device or browser details and request logs under their own privacy terms.

Advertising and consent

Kindred has applied for Google AdSense. Advertising is currently disabled on the public website while the site is being prepared for review. Reading the guides and using the planner does not require accepting advertising.

If advertising is enabled later, this policy will explain the relevant processing and choices. Google advertising may use device, browser and advertising-event information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Personalized advertising in regions covered by Google’s consent requirements will use a certified consent-management platform. A Privacy link or an essential browser-storage setting is not advertising consent.

Advertising is not placed inside profiles, private messages, connection screens, reporting tools or the developing forum. Kindred does not currently use a separate third-party analytics product on the editorial site.

Your choices and contact

You can hide or edit your community profile in the application. Requests to access, correct or delete account data can be sent privately to nadiiahonda34@gmail.com or through the Contact page. Browser choices can be removed by clearing site data.

Children

Kindred Community is intended only for people aged 18 or older. Do not create profiles for children or place children’s personal information in a profile, message or report.

Advertising is kept separate from private community activity. Public guides can be read without creating a Kindred account.
