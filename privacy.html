PRIVACY

Your story belongs to you.

This policy describes how the Kindred editorial site and adult community application process information.

Last updated: 2 September 2026

Accounts and private account data

The community application uses Google Sign-In and Firebase. When you sign in, Kindred receives your Google account identifier, display name, email address and profile image if available. Your email address is stored in a private account record and is not displayed to other members.

Community profiles

You may create an adult community profile containing a display name, city, country, introduction, interests, languages, availability and visibility choice. Signed-in members can view active profiles. Do not include an exact address, phone number, workplace, identity documents, medical information or information about children.

Connections, messages, blocks and reports

Kindred stores connection requests and their status. Private messaging is available only after the recipient accepts a request. Messages are visible to the two connected members. Blocks are used to hide profiles and prevent new messages. Reports include the selected reason, details, the reporting and reported account identifiers, status and submission time; reports are visible to the reporter and authorized moderators.

Browser storage

Kindred may use localStorage to remember interface and privacy choices. This information remains on your device until you clear the site data in your browser.

Hosting and technical logs

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, and application data is processed by Google Firebase. These providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, device or browser details and request logs under their own privacy terms.

Analytics, advertising and consent

Kindred does not currently run advertising or third-party analytics. If Google AdSense or analytics is enabled later, this policy will be updated before activation and, where required, a Google-certified consent management platform will be provided for visitors in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland.

Before advertising is activated, Kindred will explain the participating advertising providers and the purposes for which they process data. Google and its advertising partners may then use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, limit and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where a visitor has provided valid consent, personalize advertising.

Depending on a visitor's region and privacy choice, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. Relevant device, browser, consent and advertising-event information may be processed by Google and approved advertising partners under their own privacy terms. Visitors will be able to review or change eligible advertising choices through the site's certified consent interface and Google's advertising controls.

No advertising cookies will be intentionally activated through Kindred before the required consent mechanism and policy disclosures are in place.

Your choices and contact

You can hide or edit your community profile in the application. Requests to access, correct or delete account data can be sent privately to nadiiahonda34@gmail.com or through the Contact page. Browser choices can be removed by clearing site data.

Children

Kindred Community is intended only for people aged 18 or older. Do not create profiles for children or place children’s personal information in a profile, message or report.

Advertising is not shown inside the community application, private messages, connection screens or reporting tools. The policy will be reviewed before optional analytics or advertising technology is activated elsewhere.
