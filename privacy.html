PRIVACY

Your story belongs to you.

This policy explains the information used by the current Kindred public preview and the choices available to visitors and contributors.

Last updated: 31 August 2026

Information you provide

The current public preview does not enable accounts and does not transmit matcher, story, forum or report form entries to Kindred. Language, privacy choices and preview reports may be stored locally in your browser.

How information is used

Local information is used only to remember your choices and demonstrate interface states. Do not enter private contact details, identity documents, exact addresses or other sensitive information into preview forms.

Service providers

Kindred uses GitHub Pages for hosting. GitHub may process technical data such as IP address, device information and request logs under its own privacy terms. Google Firebase code is not loaded by the current public preview.

Cookies, consent and advertising

Kindred currently uses essential browser storage for language, privacy preferences and local preview reports. If Google AdSense is activated, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, device information, IP address and interaction data to deliver, measure and protect advertising. Advertising will remain disabled until the corresponding disclosures and consent controls are active.

Where required, visitors will be offered choices through a Google-certified consent management platform that supports the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. Visitors will be able to reject optional storage, revisit their choices and learn whether ads are personalized, non-personalized or limited.

Retention and choices

Local preview data remains in your browser until you clear site data. Privacy or deletion questions can be sent to nadiiahonda34@gmail.com. Kindred does not currently retain server-side copies of preview form entries.

Children

Kindred is intended for adults. We do not knowingly invite children to create accounts or arrange meetings through the service.

This page describes the current public preview. It will be reviewed before analytics, advertising or other new data-processing features are enabled.
