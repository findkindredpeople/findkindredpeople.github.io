PRIVACY

Your story belongs to you.

This policy explains the information used by the current Kindred public preview and the choices available to visitors and contributors.

Last updated: 31 August 2026

Information you provide

When you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication may provide Kindred with your account identifier, display name, email address and profile image. If you publish a story, forum topic or group request, we store the content you submit together with your user identifier and a timestamp.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, display community contributions, process group requests, prevent abuse and maintain the service. Exact location is not required by the current interface; a city name may be included when you choose to provide it.

Service providers

Kindred currently uses GitHub Pages for hosting and Google Firebase for authentication and database services. These providers may process technical data such as IP address, device information and request logs under their own terms.

Cookies, consent and advertising

Kindred currently uses essential browser storage for sign-in, security and privacy preferences. If Google AdSense is activated, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, device information, IP address and interaction data to deliver, measure and protect advertising. Advertising will remain disabled until the corresponding disclosures and consent controls are active.

Where required, visitors will be offered choices through a Google-certified consent management platform that supports the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. Visitors will be able to reject optional storage, revisit their choices and learn whether ads are personalized, non-personalized or limited.

Retention and choices

Community content may remain available until it is removed by its author or through moderation. Account deletion and data-access requests can be made through the contact page. Some records may be retained when reasonably necessary for security, legal compliance or abuse prevention.

Children

Kindred is intended for adults. We do not knowingly invite children to create accounts or arrange meetings through the service.

This page describes the current public preview. It will be reviewed before analytics, advertising or other new data-processing features are enabled.
