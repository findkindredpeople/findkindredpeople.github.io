PRIVACY

Your story belongs to you.

This policy describes what the current Kindred public preview actually does with information.

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Current preview status

Accounts and sign-in are disabled. Kindred does not currently use Google Sign-In or Firebase, and it does not receive a Google account ID, profile name or email address from visitors.

Information entered into preview tools

Matcher selections, story drafts, forum topics and other demonstration entries are not sent to a Kindred server. Preview topics and results normally exist only until the page is refreshed.

Preview reports may be saved locally in your browser so the reporting interface can be demonstrated. They are not submitted to moderators. Do not enter passwords, identity documents, exact addresses, private medical information or other sensitive data.

Browser storage

Kindred may use localStorage to remember your interface language, privacy choice and locally saved preview reports. This information remains on your device until you clear the site data in your browser.

Hosting and technical logs

Kindred is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses, device or browser details and request logs under GitHub's own privacy terms. Kindred does not receive a separate visitor database from GitHub Pages.

Analytics, advertising and consent

Kindred does not currently run advertising or third-party analytics. If Google AdSense or analytics is enabled later, this policy will be updated before activation and, where required, a Google-certified consent management platform will be provided for visitors in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland.

Before advertising is activated, Kindred will explain the participating advertising providers and the purposes for which they process data. Google and its advertising partners may then use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, limit and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where a visitor has provided valid consent, personalize advertising.

Depending on a visitor's region and privacy choice, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. Relevant device, browser, consent and advertising-event information may be processed by Google and approved advertising partners under their own privacy terms. Visitors will be able to review or change eligible advertising choices through the site's certified consent interface and Google's advertising controls.

No advertising cookies will be intentionally activated through Kindred before the required consent mechanism and policy disclosures are in place.

Your choices and contact

You can remove locally stored preview information by clearing site data for Kindred in your browser. Privacy questions can be sent privately to nadiiahonda34@gmail.com or through the Contact page.

Children

Kindred is intended for adults. The preview does not provide accounts or working tools for arranging meetings.

This policy describes the current public preview. It will be reviewed before accounts, server-side submissions, analytics, advertising or other data-processing features are enabled.
