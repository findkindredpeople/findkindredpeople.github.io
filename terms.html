Preview status and eligibility

Kindred is intended for adults and is currently a public preview. Accounts, matching, group requests, story publishing, forum publishing and report submission are not active services; interface results are demonstrations.

Your preview entries

Text entered into preview forms is not published by Kindred. Some preview choices may remain only in your browser. Do not enter content you do not have the right to use or unnecessary personal information.

Acceptable use

Do not use Kindred for harassment, exploitation, discrimination, fraud, commercial spam, illegal activity or attempts to compromise the service. Do not collect other users' information without permission.

Meetings and third parties

Sample groups and matches are fictional preview results. Kindred does not currently arrange or supervise meetings, verify identities or guarantee another person’s conduct or suitability. If future meeting tools are enabled, use independent judgment and follow the safety guidance.

Moderation and availability

The preview provides a local report interface and community rules as the foundation for future moderation. It does not yet operate a staffed in-product moderation queue. Real concerns should be sent through the private contact listed on the Contact page.
