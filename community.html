HOW IT FITS TOGETHER

Kindred is built for repeated, safer contact.

Most friendships do not begin with one dramatic moment. They grow through repeated contact, shared context, gentle follow-up and respect for each person's pace. Kindred turns that idea into a set of public guides, community spaces and private tools.

Start with a guide if you need clarity. Use Daily or Social Dice if you need a tiny action. Use the forum or meetups when you are ready to involve other people.

Safer community principles

  • Adults only. Kindred Community participation is intended for people who are at least 18.
  • Friendship, not dating. The focus is belonging, local community and shared activities.
  • City-level public details. Public posts should not include phone numbers, home addresses or sensitive personal data.
  • Public first meetings. First meetups should happen in public places, with your own transport and clear boundaries.
  • Mutual interest. Private conversation in the app is designed to begin only after both people choose it.
  • Report concerns. Unsafe, abusive, misleading or spam content can be reported and reviewed.

Common questions

Is the forum public?

Forum content is public. That is why posts should stay general and useful: city, activity, language, timing and a clear question are enough.

Can I use Kindred without signing in?

Yes. You can read guides, use the planner, roll Social Dice, check Compatibility and read safety guidance without an account. Posting, meetups and private community features require sign-in.

What makes a good meetup?

A good first meetup is specific, public and simple: a short walk, coffee near a library, a language exchange in a public place or a casual game. Avoid exact private addresses and keep expectations modest.
